Root Locus Explorer

Move a PID gain and watch the root locus, the closed-loop poles, and the step response change together. MATLAB's Control System Designer shows the locus but hides why it moves; this puts the pole location and the behavior on one screen.

  • Kp, Ki, Kd and overall gain K, positive or negative, with exact numeric entry
  • Connected locus branches, breakaway points, imaginary-axis crossings and asymptotes
  • Drag a closed-loop pole along its branch and K follows
  • Characteristic polynomial, live pole sum and product, and a Routh–Hurwitz table that flags systems with all-positive coefficients that are still unstable
  • Step response and control effort, with a ghost trace to compare changes
  • Paste a MATLAB tf, or export MATLAB code, JSON, or a shareable link

Zero-G Flight Simulator

A plane flies a curved trajectory and the simulator reports what the pilot would feel: elevation, horizontal and vertical speed, angle, vertical acceleration, and G-force.

Runs on your computer (Python 3, no extra packages): python Zero_G.py

PID Demo

My first simulator: a dot chases a target while sliders for Kp, Ki, Kd, and damping change how it gets there. A live readout shows position, velocity, acceleration, the integral term, and the controller output, and the dot leaves a trail so overshoot and oscillation are easy to see.

Runs on your computer (Python 3, no extra packages): python pid_demo.py

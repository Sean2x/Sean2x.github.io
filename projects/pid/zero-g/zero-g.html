A real zero-g flight is a parabola: the plane pulls up at about 1.8 g, then the pilot unloads the wings so nothing pushes on the plane but gravity (the plane and everyone inside fall together: 0 g), then pulls out. Felt gravity here is the load factor n, the lift pushing on the plane as a multiple of its weight. Thrust is assumed to cancel drag, so speed only trades with altitude.

Oct 9, 2026

Built a PID root locus explorer

Move a PID gain and watch the root locus, the closed-loop poles, and the step response change together. MATLAB shows the locus but hides why it moves, so I made a tool that shows the link between pole location and behavior. It also runs a Routh–Hurwitz check, which catches systems where every coefficient is positive but poles are still in the right half-plane.
